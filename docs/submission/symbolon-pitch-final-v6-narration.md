# Symbolon pitch narration

English narration for 12 slides. Target: approximately 2 minutes at about 129 words per minute. Total 258 words. This is a timing estimate; no recorded rehearsal establishes the duration.

## 1. Symbolon

Target: 7 seconds

Imagine borrowing against assets without knowing your final interest bill. That is the problem behind Symbolon.

## 2. Repayment uncertainty

Target: 10 seconds

Floating rates can change before repayment. A treasury team financing a known period still cannot lock its interest budget.

## 3. Rates can move

Target: 10 seconds

Rate uncertainty affects lenders too. This Aave USDC lending series moves against an illustrative fixed line. Symbolon lets counterparties agree their rate before settlement.

## 4. A fixed bill before settlement

Target: 13 seconds

Symbolon lets lenders offer fixed APRs. One thousand for thirty days at five point two percent means a contractual repayment of one thousand four point three three.

## 5. Why Canton

Target: 12 seconds

Canton applies the agreed rules and exchanges cash against collateral atomically. Requests reach Symbolon users. Quotes and positions stay bilateral. Issuers and operators remain trust dependencies.

## 6. The financing flow

Target: 12 seconds

Publish once. Lenders quote directly with reserved cash. The borrower compares offers, accepts one, and later repays to recover collateral.

## 7. Collateral still needs attention

Target: 10 seconds

Collateral risk remains. Falling prices can trigger a margin call, requiring a top-up. Even early repayment still costs the full agreed amount.

## 8. What we have demonstrated

Target: 12 seconds

The direct financing flow has completed on DevNet with simulated assets. One borrower episode and roughly three to five informal explorers provide early feedback. Institutional adoption remains unvalidated.

## 9. Business model

Target: 10 seconds

The proposed fee is zero point one percent of interest from each side. For five thousand in interest, Symbolon would receive ten. The demo currently collects nothing.

## 10. The next 90 days

Target: 12 seconds

Over ninety days, we plan operator interviews and an external borrower and lender rehearsal. Native assets and operational approval come before a real asset pilot.

## 11. Team

Target: 5 seconds

I'm albary, building Symbolon through EndPx.

## 12. Thank you

Target: 7 seconds

Try the app, read the docs, and inspect the source. Help shape the first treasury rehearsal. Thank you.
