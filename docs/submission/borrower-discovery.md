## Follow-up with the same borrower

1. What LTV would have felt comfortable, and why? Did you want less debt for safety, or more borrowing capacity?
2. Was closing after two months planned from the start, or did a market/rate change trigger it?
3. What were the borrowing rate and debt amount at entry and close, if you remember? Did any change affect your decision or budget?
4. How often did you monitor the position, and what action did you take when health changed?
5. After explaining fixed maturity and full agreed repayment even on early closure: which terms would you accept, and which would stop you using the workflow?

## Next two interviews

Seek two additional relevant operators. Prioritize at least one treasury operator at a small or mid-sized digital-asset fund with Canton exposure or a concrete adoption plan. Record actual role and asset/network fit rather than assuming either. Start with a real episode before describing Symbolon.

Use a 15–20 minute conversation:

1. Tell me about the last time you borrowed against collateral: which assets, platform, amount/LTV and purpose?
2. How did you estimate the repayment cost, and what actually happened while the position was open?
3. Did a rate change, collateral-price change or another event alter your budget or decision? What changed, by how much, and what did you do?
4. Why and when did you close? How much flexibility did you need to repay early or extend?
5. What information about that financing would you prefer to keep private, and from whom?
6. Present Symbolon's fixed rate, term and contractual repayment, plus remaining collateral risk and full early repayment. Compared with their actual episode, what would be useful or unacceptable?
7. What must be true about price, lender availability, supported assets, participant/custody access and operational approval before they could try a simulated rehearsal?
8. Would they take a concrete next step? Record an agreed walkthrough or rehearsal separately from general interest. Ask about pricing only with a specific useful workflow and the person who controls that budget.

For each interview, record date, anonymized role, permission for attribution, episode facts, quantified consequences if known, objections, ICP fit and any specific next commitment. Separate verbatim quotes from paraphrases. Unknown amounts and dates stay unknown.
