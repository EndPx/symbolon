# Features

Symbolon brings quote comparison, settlement and collateral management into one financing workspace.

## Publish Once, Let Lenders Quote Directly

Unauthenticated visitors see a minimal listing. After one publication consent, authenticated connected parties see borrower identity and full request terms and can quote directly with their own cash. No lender registration or request-access approval step is needed.

**Example:** Alex publishes 1,000 USDCx for 30 days. Blair reads those terms while connected and funds a 7% offer. Publication records the OpenRequest but does not transfer cash or pledge collateral.

## Compare fixed-rate offers

Connected lenders independently price the same published request. Received offers show APR, term interest and total repayment together.

**Example:** Blair quotes 7% and Casey quotes 7.5% for 1,000 over 30 days. Alex compares approximately 1,005.83 and 1,006.25 total repayment, then reviews the full terms of the preferred offer.

## Know the repayment before settlement

The chosen rate and contractual repayment are fixed for that agreement. Paying early does not reduce the agreed interest.

**Example:** if the accepted repayment is approximately 1,005.83, closing on day 10 still requires the full agreed amount rather than ten days of interest.

## Settle cash and collateral together

Acceptance exchanges reserved lender cash and borrower collateral in one ledger transaction. A request by itself does not transfer assets.

**Example:** Alex receives 1,000 USDCx while 0.0250 cBTC-demo transfers to Blair and remains locked under the position.

## Monitor collateral health

Positions show coverage, the agreed margin boundary and the price at which a margin call can become available. A top-up can restore coverage.

**Example:** 0.0250 collateral at 60,000 is worth 1,500. For 1,000 principal and 105% maintenance cover, health is about 1.43. At 36,000 it becomes about 0.86.

## Keep quotes and positions bilateral

Full published request terms are shared by consent with all authenticated connected parties. Quote APRs and positions remain bilateral. Each quote is shared with its borrower and lender; the winning position has its own counterparties.

**Example:** Casey can see Casey's offer and any cancellation affecting it, but does not automatically receive Blair's rate or Alex and Blair's resulting position.

## Review balances and completed positions

Portfolio separates issuer-specific holdings, open positions and closed records. A same-symbol asset from another issuer is shown separately.

**Example:** USDCx issued by two different parties appears as two balances. Only the issuer specified in the agreement can fund or repay that agreement.
